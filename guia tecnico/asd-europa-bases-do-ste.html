What Is Simplified Technical English (STE)?

Clearer writing. Safer products. Fewer misunderstandings.

  1. ASD
  2. Standards & Specifications
  3. Simplified Technical English
  4. What are the basics of Simplified Technical English?

ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (STE) is an international standard for writing clear, unambiguous technical content. ASD-STE100 consists of restricted writing rules and a limited (controlled) vocabulary.

Originally developed for civil aviation, it is now used across industries such as aerospace, defence, automotive, machinery, pharmaceuticals, and IT – wherever safety, compliance, and clarity matter. STE is now a powerful tool for diverse industries.

Learn more about the history of STE

Created in the 1980s by the European aerospace industry under AECMA to improve the clarity of maintenance manuals in civil aviation. Since 1983, the STE Maintenance Group (STEMG) has been working to make technical English clear, consistent, and safe – for everyone, everywhere.

Learn more

The two core components of ASD-STE100

Writing rules

  • Use the active voice
  • One instruction per sentence
  • No unnecessary synonyms or jargon
  • Minimum number of words per sentence
  • Consistency across documents and teams


A controlled dictionary

  • A dictionary of approximately 900 approved words
  • Each approved word has a single, defined meaning

Example: Instead of “begin,” “commence", or “initiate,” STE uses only “start”.

Why use STE?

Ambiguous instructions can lead to loss of time, money, and even lives. STE prevents  this risk by reducing complexity and standardising vocabulary.

It is especially effective for:

  • Technical manuals and service instructions
  • Multilingual environments
  • Safety-critical documentation
  • Translations
  • AI-readiness and data reuse
